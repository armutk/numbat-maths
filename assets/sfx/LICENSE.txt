Sound effects in this folder are derived from Kenney (www.kenney.nl) audio packs,
released under Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal (public domain dedication):
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Sources:
  Kenney "Interface Sounds"  https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
  Kenney "UI Audio"          https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-audio
  Kenney "Impact Sounds"     https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds

Modifications: trimmed, pitch-shifted/layered/low-passed in places, normalised to
-3 dBFS peak and converted to 16-bit PCM mono 24000 Hz WAV. The per-sound source
files are listed in manifest.json ("src"). Credit is not required but appreciated.
